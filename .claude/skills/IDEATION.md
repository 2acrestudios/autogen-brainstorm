# Ideation Skill

Generate creative ideas and solutions using structured brainstorming techniques.

## When to Use This Skill

- Starting a new project or feature
- Overcoming creative blocks
- Exploring multiple solution approaches
- Generating marketing campaign ideas
- Developing product concepts

## How This Skill Works

This skill uses proven ideation techniques to generate diverse, creative solutions:

1. **SCAMPER Technique**
   - Substitute: What can be replaced?
   - Combine: What can be merged?
   - Adapt: What can be modified?
   - Modify: What can be changed?
   - Put to other uses: How else can it be used?
   - Eliminate: What can be removed?
   - Reverse: What can be flipped?

2. **Mind Mapping**
   - Start with central concept
   - Branch into related themes
   - Connect related ideas
   - Identify patterns and opportunities

3. **Six Thinking Hats**
   - White Hat: Facts and information
   - Red Hat: Emotions and feelings
   - Black Hat: Risks and challenges
   - Yellow Hat: Benefits and optimism
   - Green Hat: Creativity and alternatives
   - Blue Hat: Process and control

## Example Usage

**User:** "I need ideas for a sustainable product launch campaign"

**Ideation Skill Response:**

### SCAMPER Analysis

**Substitute:** Replace traditional advertising with influencer partnerships
**Combine:** Merge product launch with sustainability workshop series
**Adapt:** Adapt successful eco-campaigns from other industries
**Modify:** Change launch timing to align with Earth Day
**Put to other uses:** Use packaging as educational material
**Eliminate:** Remove single-use materials from all promotional items
**Reverse:** Start with customer stories, then reveal product

### Mind Map Branches
- Digital-First Campaign
  - Social media challenges
  - Virtual launch event
  - AR product demonstrations
- Community Engagement
  - Local partnerships
  - Sustainability pledges
  - User-generated content
- Educational Content
  - Behind-the-scenes sustainability
  - Impact metrics dashboard
  - Carbon footprint calculator

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be Specific**: Provide clear context about your challenge
2. **Embrace Quantity**: More ideas lead to better quality options
3. **Defer Judgment**: Don't critique ideas during generation phase
4. **Build on Ideas**: Use generated concepts as springboards
5. **Combine Approaches**: Mix techniques for richer results
